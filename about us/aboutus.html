AVR Service Story

AVR DABBA — Delivered With Care

AVR DABBA was born from a simple thought — every child deserves to enjoy a fresh, healthy, homemade meal at school. As a parent, preparing a child's lunch every morning can be challenging. Busy work schedules, early mornings, and daily responsibilities can sometimes make it difficult to prepare and personally deliver a homemade meal to school. We understood that the solution was not to replace homemade food, but to make it easier for parents to get that homemade food to their children. That is how AVR DABBA began. With AVR DABBA, parents prepare fresh and healthy food at home with love. Our delivery partner carefully picks up the packed lunch from the parent's home and delivers it safely and on time to the child's school. Our goal is simple: “You cook with love. We deliver with care.” We want parents to have peace of mind knowing that their children can enjoy the food prepared at home, even when parents cannot personally take it to school. We believe that homemade food is more than just a meal — it is a feeling of home, care and love. AVR DABBA is built around three important values: Homemade Goodness with food prepared by parents with love, Safety & Hygiene through careful handling and reliable delivery, and Convenience by helping busy parents save time and reduce their morning rush. Our mission is to build a reliable bridge between parents' kitchens and children's schools. We want to make school-time meals easier for parents and happier for children by bringing the taste, care and comfort of home closer to them. We envision a future where no busy parent has to worry about how their homemade lunch will reach their child at school. AVR DABBA aims to become a trusted home-to-school food delivery service, connecting families with convenience while keeping homemade food at the heart of every meal. Every dabba we deliver represents something special — a parent's effort, a family's love and a child's happiness. Because homemade is best.
