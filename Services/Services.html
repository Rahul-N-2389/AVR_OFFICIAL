What Is Our Service?

  • AVR DABBA provides a dedicated lunch box pickup and delivery service for parents and school children across Hyderabad. We connect parents’ kitchens with their children’s schools by picking up ready lunch boxes from their homes and delivering them safely to the school.
  • Our delivery partners collect packed lunch boxes from parents’ homes between 9:30 AM and 11:30 AM. Pickup times are planned based on the distance between your home and your child’s school. The lunch boxes are carried safely in insulated thermal bags to help keep the food warm and fresh. We aim to deliver the lunch box to the school before lunch time, so it reaches the child in time for their lunch break.
DAILY OPERATIONS ROUTINE

How It Works — Daily Operations Routine

Every minute is planned for maximum safety, nutrition retention, and zero interruption to classes.

1

CHOOSE YOUR PLAN & BOOK

Select a suitable delivery plan and book your lunch box service through WhatsApp or our website in just a few simple steps.

2

COOK FRESH & GET DOORSTEP PICKUP

Prepare your child’s homemade lunch at a convenient time, and our delivery executive will collect the packed lunch box from your doorstep between 9:30 AM and 11:30 AM.

3

SAFE & INSULATED TRANSIT

Your lunch box is carefully secured and transported in insulated bags to help maintain its freshness and temperature during delivery.

4

SCHOOL DROP-OFF & HANDOVER

Our delivery executive reaches the school and hands over the lunch boxes through the designated drop-off process for safe and organized delivery.

5

HOT HOMEMADE MEAL AT SCHOOL

At lunchtime, your child receives their homemade meal at school—bringing the comfort and care of home to their lunch break.
